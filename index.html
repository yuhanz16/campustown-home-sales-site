Median sale price by college town

The median sale price in each city, from the College-town home sales dataset. Cities are listed from highest to lowest median.

Rule: sales under $10,000 are left out. Every other sale counts once, including sales at the same address. The median is the middle price when the sales are sorted; with an even number of sales it is halfway between the two middle prices, and it is shown rounded to the nearest dollar.

The data description sheet also lists city medians. It counts sales of $1,000 or more, not $10,000 or more, so its figures can differ slightly from these.

Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.

The figures come from data/city_median_sale_prices.csv, which scripts/make_city_median_prices.py computes from data/college_town_home_sales.csv.
